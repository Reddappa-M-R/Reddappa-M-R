Apache Spark & Big Data

Architecting Distributed Computing at Scale

1. Spark Architecture

Understanding the Driver and Executor relationship is fundamental. In a cluster, the driver orchestrates the execution plan while executors handle the physical data processing tasks.

Pro Tip: Always monitor your Spark UI to check for "Stragglers"—these are executors taking significantly longer than others, often caused by severe data skew.

2. Partitioning & Shuffling

Shuffling is the most expensive operation in Spark because it involves moving data across the network between executors. Minimizing it is the key to performance.

  • Coalesce vs Repartition: Use coalesce() to reduce partition count without a full shuffle. Use repartition() only when you need to increase partitions or balance data.
  • Broadcast Joins: When joining a massive fact table with a small dimension table, use a broadcast join to send the small table to every executor, eliminating the need for a shuffle.

3. Efficient Data Formats

Why is Parquet the industry standard? As a columnar format, it enables "Predicate Pushdown"—Spark can skip entire chunks of data by reading only the specific columns and rows required by your query.
